Also: ORBIT guide data file `brain-data.json` must be in the target's Copy Bundle Resources (drag it in with the Swift files and tick the target).

Add these keys to the target's Info tab (Xcode > target > Info) so the app can use the features below.

NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription   "ORBIT shows your position on the live map while the screen is open."
NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription     "ORBIT saves your edited travel photos to your library."
ORBIT_API_URL (String)                 Your backend address, for example https://api.example.com

Local development only (Simulator talking to a backend on your Mac over http://localhost:8080):
App Transport Security Settings > Allow Local Networking = YES
Do not ship a build with http:// addresses.
