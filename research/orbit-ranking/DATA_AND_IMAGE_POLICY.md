# ORBIT data and image policy

- Every destination, hotel, restaurant and transport record needs a stable ID, source, location and verification date.
- Hotel observations must identify room type, occupancy, stay date and taxes/fees when known.
- Restaurant observations must identify dish, portion and menu date.
- Transport observations must identify route, mode, fare type (fixed/negotiated), date and source.
- Display data as verified, community-reported, estimated, outdated or unknown. Never imply a price is guaranteed or live unless a live provider confirms it.
- Currency conversions must include exchange-rate source and timestamp.
- For images, record the exact source page, direct file URL, author, licence name/URL, attribution text and review date. Wikimedia Commons search results are candidates, not automatic permission. Manually check each file's licence and relevance.
- Respect website terms and data licences. Do not bypass access controls or assume public webpages grant reuse rights.
- Obtain explicit consent before publishing traveller images or location data. Strip EXIF GPS unless the contributor explicitly opts in.
