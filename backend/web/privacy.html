1. What this policy covers

ORBIT is a travel planning app for Bangladesh. This page explains what information the app and its server handle, why, who else receives it, and the choices you have. You can use the planner, explorer, map and photo editor without an account. An account is needed for the AI guide, syncing, the leaderboard and spot submissions.

3. Permissions the app asks for

Location is requested only when you open the live map, and only while the app is in use. The app does not track you in the background. Photos / camera: choosing a photo uses the system photo picker, and taking one opens your phone's own camera app, so ORBIT does not need broad access to your library. Saving an edited photo asks for permission only on older Android versions. You can refuse or revoke any permission in your phone settings. Features that need it simply stay unavailable.

4. Agreements you give at sign-up

Required: Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy. Optional, and off unless you turn them on: (a) AI guide, which allows your messages to be processed by the AI provider; (b) location sharing with the guide; (c) appearing on the public leaderboard with your display name, number of spots and districts. Change these any time under Account, Data and permissions.

5. Who else receives information

  • AI provider: none by default, because the built-in guide runs on your phone. If the optional cloud guide is enabled and you agree, the provider receives your message, recent chat turns, and the summary of your visits and saved notes needed to answer, and may process them outside Bangladesh.
  • Map provider: on Android the live map uses Google Maps, and on iOS it uses Apple Maps. Your device shares location and network details with them under their own policies.
  • Other travellers: see only your display name and visit counts, and only if you joined the leaderboard.
  • We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

6. How long we keep it

Account data and visits are kept until you delete them or your account. Guide memory notes stay on your phone until you delete them. Verification codes expire after 10 minutes. Login sessions expire after 7 days.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: Account, Download my data.
  • Correct it: edit visits and notes in the app, or ask us.
  • Delete it: Account, Delete account removes your account and visits from our server. Guide notes can be deleted in Account. Approved community spots stay, with the link to you removed.
  • Withdraw any optional agreement at any time.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed, login tokens are signed and expire, and the server should only be run behind HTTPS. Login and signup attempts are rate limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

9. Children

ORBIT is not meant for people under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will show the new version and ask you to accept it again. The version date is at the top of this page.
